This week's scorecard

Rejection rate is the only score that matters

Rejection rate is the share of stock we refuse at the stall. Low is good — it means what they offer us is already up to standard. Above 15% for two consecutive weeks and the stall comes off the list.

Why only two

Depth beats breadth at this size

Spreading across ten stalls would look impressive and would quietly destroy the thing we sell. Two relationships mean the vendors know our checker by name, know exactly what gets refused, and have enough weekly volume at stake to care.
